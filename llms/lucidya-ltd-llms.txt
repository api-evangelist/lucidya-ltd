# Lucidya

> Lucidya is an AI-native customer experience management (CXM) platform for social
> listening, unified customer data, omnichannel engagement, and AI text analysis,
> with deep Arabic-language and MENA-market capabilities. Its public developer
> platform exposes a suite of RESTful APIs across six products.

## API Products

- [Social Listening API](https://docs.lucidya.com/docs/Social-Listening-api/wainb9dny9w1y-lucidya-social-listening-public-api): Monitor and retrieve data across social platforms (X, Instagram, Intercom, and more); filters, alerts, usage.
- [AI API](https://docs.lucidya.com/docs/ai-api/zl9th61r4qjdz-lucidya-public-ai-api): Sentiment analysis, Arabic dialect detection, theme/sub-theme classification, domain categorization, audio transcription.
- [CDP API](https://docs.lucidya.com/docs/cdp-api/ns8sfqyr1dgw6-cdp-customer-data-platform-api): Unified customer profiles, interaction histories, and survey data from the Customer Data Platform.
- [OmniChannel API](https://docs.lucidya.com/docs/omnichannel-api/d1ow4caem6i5x-omni-channel-api): Retrieve widget data across connected channels (social, chat, rating, mail, call).
- [OmniServe Analytics API](https://docs.lucidya.com/docs/omniserve/mjhuba7fdw882-omni-serve-analytics-api): Analytics for Inbox, SLAs, Agents, and In-Chat Survey (KPIs, time-series, distributions).
- [Webhooks](https://docs.lucidya.com/docs/webhooks/gjni0qgds8vl8-get-started): Real-time push notifications when events/conditions are met across monitors.
